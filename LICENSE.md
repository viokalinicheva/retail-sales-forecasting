# Source-data boundary

The supplied course CSV and original Census archives are not redistributed, and this license does not claim ownership of the underlying government statistics. The executable workflow reconstructs the exact 354 observations directly from four original Census archives. Source identity, nominal USD-million units, seasonal adjustment, and fixed July 2021 revision vintage are verified and documented in sources.json and README.md.

Census DS027, page 9, describes the U.S. copyright status of employee-created data/works, with stated exceptions: https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf .
